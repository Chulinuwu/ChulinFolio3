# Mascot: An-An

Source of truth for every illustration that includes the mascot. Generated art must match `model-sheet.png` and follow these rules; reject any image that breaks one.

## Identity
- Hair: deep maroon `#5F3236`, shade `#3E2528`. Shoulder length, never longer.
- Bangs: blunt and heavy, covering the eyebrows. Two side locks framing the face down to the chin.
- Ponytail: one low side ponytail on HER right (viewer's left in front view), from behind the ear to the shoulder, tied with a black ribbon bow.
- Face: deadpan, sleepy half-lidded eyes `#281718` with one tiny highlight, small pouty `^` mouth, no nose, skin `#F1DBCB`, faint blush.
- Headphones: exactly one black over-ear pair, either around the neck (default) or on the ears (focused work). Never both.
- Outfit: oversized white-grey `#F2F1ED` (shade `#D9D7D2`) knit turtleneck with sleeves covering the hands; short charcoal `#392727` pleated skirt; white ribbed socks `#F4F2EE` to mid-calf; dark brown `#3A2A28` loafers.
- Proportions: chibi, 3 heads tall.
- Personality: low-energy, deadpan, cozy introvert developer. Expressions stay understated; a "big" reaction is a tiny smile or a blush.

## Rendering
Hand-drawn sticker style: a thick, wobbly ink outline `#291A1A`; flat fills with exactly one hard shade step; sparse detail. No gradients, glow, gloss, texture or 3D shading.

Palette (the whole world, not just the mascot): white `#FFFFFF`, paper `#FAFAF8`, light grey `#D9D7D2`, mid grey `#7A6F6E`, sage `#8FB08A`, deep green `#3F6B4A`, maroon `#5F3236`, ink `#291A1A`, pale blossom pink `#F6C9D3`. No yellow, gold, amber, orange or cream anywhere, including lamps, trophies and skin highlights.
